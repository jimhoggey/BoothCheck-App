Lightkey and Booth Check on the MacBook: how to start, how to finish, and what to do if something’s wrong.

Leave everything plugged in: the charger, and the USB cable from the MacBook to the blue ENTTEC Open DMX box.

Start

  1. Open the MacBook and press the power button.

  2. Type the password. It’s on the MacBook.

  3. Wait, and don’t touch anything. A big sign says so. If it asks you to replug, pull the USB cable out of the blue box and plug it back in.

  4. Test the Stream Deck: press Default, then press it again. Default comes on with Lightkey. Off, then on again: the deck is connected.

  5. Ready. Next, pick a worship look. Press Next on the Stream Deck for the worship colours, for example Ocean.

Finish

  1. Click the Booth Check icon (top right), then Shut down the booth. Can’t see it? Move the pointer to the top of the screen. If Lightkey asks to save, click Don’t Save.

  2. Close the lid.

If the lights run away

Unplug the Stream Deck’s USB cable. Don’t press anything else.

Run the rest from Lightkey’s panel with the mouse, and tell Fynn afterwards.

Something not working?

Lightkey asks to Authenticate

Tap Authenticate on the Touch Bar (or click it), and type the password again.

Lightkey shows its list of projects, or nothing

Click the newest BackupChurch_modular. Can’t see Lightkey? Click its icon in the Dock.

The Booth Check icon (top right) shows a triangle or a cross

Click it. Each item says what to do.

Stream Deck keys do nothing, then all fire at once

Stop pressing. Click the Stream Deck app once.

A key is lit but its cue isn’t on, or the other way round

Quit the Stream Deck app: Booth Check reopens it in a few seconds with every key clean. Lightkey’s panel is always right.

The Stream Deck does nothing at all

Pick the newest “Lightkey … Service” profile, or quit and reopen the Stream Deck app.

Cues change in Lightkey, but no lights come on

Reseat the DMX USB cable at both ends, then quit and reopen Lightkey.

Old cues, wrong looks or missing buttons

File → Open Recent → the newest BackupChurch_modular.

The screen is black, or the Mac went to sleep

Plug the charger in, then press a key.

Anything else

Restart the MacBook (Apple menu → Restart), then Start from step 2. Still stuck? Send Fynn a photo of the screen and what you tried.
