Church lighting

Lightkey and Booth Check on the MacBook. Shut the MacBook down after every use.

Leave everything plugged in: the charger, and the USB cable from the MacBook to the blue ENTTEC Open DMX box.

Start

  1. Open the MacBook and press the power button.

  2. Type the password. It’s written on the MacBook.

  3. Wait. Don’t click anything yet. Booth Check finds the DMX box, then opens Lightkey by itself.

  4. In Lightkey, click the most recent project: BackupChurch_modular_v30

  5. Tap Authenticate on the Touch Bar, and type the password again.

  6. The lights are working.

Finish

  1. Apple menu → Shut Down.

  2. Close the lid. Leave everything plugged in.

No lights?

Check the USB cable is in the blue box, then restart the MacBook.
