OPEN BOOTH CHECK ON A NEW MAC

macOS blocks Booth Check the first time ("could not be verified") because it
isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account. Do this once per Mac.


1. Put Booth Check in Applications.

2. Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Booth Check.app"

3. Open Booth Check.


If it's not in Applications, type the start of the command with a space at
the end, drag Booth Check into the Terminal window, then press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine


No Terminal? Try to open Booth Check once, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".


Once it's open: Booth Check lives in the menu bar at the top right (no Dock
icon). Click its icon, then "Open Booth Check" to see the window.
